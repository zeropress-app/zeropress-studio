# Studio UI copy

Apply the same punctuation policy to English and Korean. Decide by the text's
role on screen, not just its translation key or whether it is a full sentence.

- Omit terminal periods from headings, navigation, buttons, short field and
  status labels, and brief brand copy.
- Use periods for explanatory, instructional, validation, and feedback
  sentences. This includes sentence-length acknowledgements and accessibility
  explanations.
- Keep question marks on questions, ellipses for ongoing activity, and colons
  or other punctuation that carry meaning.
- Keep notice titles separate from their explanatory bodies. A title has no
  terminal period; an instruction beneath it does.
- Preserve punctuation in URLs, filenames, code, and user-authored content.

For example, `Sign in to Studio` is a heading,
`Enter your email and password to continue.` is an instruction, and
`More room for the things you want to say` is brand copy.
